# Behavioral / STAR Story Bank

Reusable answers for VP-level behavioral rounds. Each story is written once in **STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) and mapped to the questions it answers. Keep results quantified — pull numbers from [`../profile/candidate-profile.md`](../profile/candidate-profile.md).

> Tip: most VP behavioral questions map to 6–8 core stories. Build those deeply; don't invent a story per question.

## Story index → question mapping

| Story | Best for questions about |
|---|---|
| S1. Shipping production AI agents (IAH/TestBOT) | Delivering AI from scratch; ambiguity; technical judgment |
| S2. Enterprise AI strategy + Gemini rollout | Strategy; C-suite partnership; org-wide change |
| S3. IAH multi-tenant AI platform delivery | Cross-functional; tenancy/governance; high stakes |
| S4. Cost governance / >$200K savings | Business impact; ownership; influence without authority |
| S5. Regulated-industry delivery (OT/SCADA, FDA) | Risk; safety; compliance; slowing down to go fast |
| S6. Org design / scaling teams | People leadership; hiring; distributed teams |
| S7. A failure / thing you'd do differently | Failure; learning; humility |
| S8. Conflict / disagreement with a peer or exec | Conflict; managing up; influence |
