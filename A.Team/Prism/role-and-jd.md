## Read on the mission (positioning)

The posting is not asking for an agent *builder*. It's asking for someone who owns the layer **beneath**
agent creation: the contract every agent declares, the registry it joins, the routing that connects it, and
the guarantees that make its output trustworthy and repeatable. That is exactly the AI control plane at
CruxOCM — plus 15 years of build/release/gating work, which is the same discipline (guarantee correctness
and reproducibility of generated output) applied to a slower generator.

**Angle:** lead with *ontology + registration + governed orchestration*, not with "I built agents."
Their stated bottleneck — semantic correctness, reproducibility, architectural soundness — is the exact
problem an AWS Redshift build/release owner solves for a living.
