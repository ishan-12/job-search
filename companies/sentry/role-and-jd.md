## Full JD (captured from Ashby posting API)

**About the role:** Sentry's Developer Infrastructure team is a company-wide force multiplier — every
engineer depends on its tooling: CI/CD systems, development environments, and golden paths. As Sentry
moves into a world where developers work alongside multiple coding agents in parallel, Dev Infra shapes
how that new development lifecycle works. The EM leads a team building the platform that supports both
developers and the agents alongside them: **reproducible cloud-based dev environments, fast/trustworthy
CI/CD, queryable development-lifecycle context, and the infrastructure for background agents.** Owns
technical vision, team health, system reliability, and cross-functional partnership. Strong growth
opportunity — expected to take on additional charters and teams as Dev Infra expands.

### In this role you will
- Lead a team building the platform that accelerates every engineer at Sentry.
- Drive the evolution of the Dev Infra platform: reproducible cloud environments, CI/CD, development-lifecycle context, and the background-agent platform.
- Own technical direction and architecture — remote dev environments, selective testing, golden paths, agent orchestration.
- Nurture and grow engineers via coaching, mentorship, and career development.
- Foster an inclusive, high-performing team culture (ownership, learning, delivery).
- Collaborate with product and platform teams to make AI-assisted dev workflows fast, safe, and observable.
- Own reliability, performance, and adoption of the team's roadmap.

### Qualifications
- 10+ years software engineering, ideally developer infrastructure, build/CI systems, platform engineering, or distributed systems.
- 4+ years engineering management, including growing/managing teams across **multiple charters**.
- TL or EM leading architecture and long-term evolution of developer-facing platforms (CI/CD, dev environments, build infra).
- Familiarity with modern CI/CD (**GitHub Actions, ArgoCD**, or similar), cloud-based dev environments, container/VM orchestration.
- Working knowledge of AI-assisted development workflows and **a point of view on how coding agents change dev-infra requirements**.
- Strong technical communication; cross-team & leadership collaboration.
- Proven delivery/ownership of multi-year engineering initiatives.
- Comfortable aligning product, infra, and platform priorities across org boundaries.
