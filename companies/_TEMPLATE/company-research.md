A dealbreaker in `scout/profiles/criteria.md`: **at least 24 months of runway,
unless default-alive** (monthly ARR exceeds monthly burn). This is almost never
in the job posting, so it will not be caught by scoring — it has to be checked
here, and it is cheapest to check first.

- **Last round:** <amount, series, date>

  recent press is the strongest negative signal available>
- **Verdict:** ☐ passes ☐ fails ☐ can't tell

If it **fails**, stop here and do not tailor a resume — that is the cost this
gate exists to avoid. If it's **can't tell**, proceed but put it at the top of
the questions you ask the recruiter on the first call.

Useful sources: Crunchbase, the company's own funding announcement, LinkedIn
headcount over time, Layoffs.fyi.
