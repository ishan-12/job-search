## The Role (from the JD)

Lead the **Infrastructure Platform team** building and operating the foundational cloud infrastructure that powers
engineering velocity org-wide. Ensure product teams can reliably provision, deploy, and operate at scale while
meeting strict security and compliance requirements. Define and execute the strategy for internal infrastructure
platforms — fast iteration without compromising reliability or control.

**Your impact:**
- Deliver a scalable **internal infrastructure platform on public cloud** so teams provision/operate efficiently.
- Establish/evolve **Kubernetes-based platform capabilities** for HA, production-grade workloads at scale.
- Achieve and maintain **99.9% uptime SLOs**.
- Faster **idea → production** — improve provisioning workflows, reduce operational friction.
- Meet **SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001** via robust access control and provisioning standards.
- Secure, reliable foundation for **CI/CD**; minimize operational risk.

**Requirements:**
- Building/operating **internal infra platforms on public cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure)** — provisioning, scaling, reliability.
- **Led/managed teams of SW/infra engineers 3+ yrs** (hire, develop, retain).
- **Ownership of Kubernetes at scale — built/led a K8s-based internal platform from the ground up.**
- Effective in **startup** environments — ambiguity, lean teams, foundational systems.

**Preferred:**
- **SOC 2 Type 2 + ISO 27001** in infra provisioning/access control.
- CI/CD ecosystems + supporting infra (**runners, secrets management, cloud networking**).
- Security best practices in software delivery (**secure artifact management**, compliance).
- **Blockchain / Web3** exposure.
- Globally distributed, **remote-first** teams.
- Infra automation via **Go-based tooling or GitHub Actions**.
