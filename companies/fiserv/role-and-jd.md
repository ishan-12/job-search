## What the role is

Lead the **enterprise strategy for DevOps, Platform Engineering, Cloud Operations, SRE, and AI-enabled
infrastructure** across Fiserv, partnering with Engineering, Product, Architecture, Security, Data Science,
and Infrastructure.

**What you'll do (their order):**
1. Enterprise DevOps/SRE/platform/cloud-ops strategy for scalable, resilient, secure platforms
2. Build and develop high-performing DevOps, SRE, cloud engineering and platform teams
3. Modernize CI/CD, release management, environment management, infrastructure automation, cloud-native practices
4. Establish governance, engineering standards, and best practices for delivery and operational excellence
5. **Leverage GenAI, ML, AIOps and MLOps** to automate infrastructure management, incident response, RCA, monitoring, capacity planning
6. Reliability engineering: SLOs, SLAs, KPIs, observability standards, DR readiness, resilience programs
7. DevSecOps with InfoSec: automated security controls, vulnerability management, compliance across the SDLC
8. Strategic technology investments, budgets, vendor relationships, executive partnerships
