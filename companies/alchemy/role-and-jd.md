## The Role (from the JD)

Own the vision, strategy, and execution of one of the most sophisticated, high-throughput **distributed systems**
in the blockchain ecosystem. Lead multiple teams responsible for the core platform powering Alchemy's products,
where **reliability, performance, and cost efficiency at massive scale** are mission-critical. Set long-term
technical direction; partner with Product and Executive Leadership; keep Alchemy at the frontier of Web3 infra.

**What you'll do:**
- Lead and scale **multiple engineering teams** across **Cloud Infrastructure, Platform, Data Platform, and
  Internal Tooling** — including distributed and international teams.
- Define and own **platform & infrastructure strategy** — scalability, reliability, performance, cost efficiency.
- Senior technical leader influencing **architecture / systems design for large-scale mission-critical
  distributed systems**.
- Partner with **Product, Security, Finance, and Executive Leadership**; align platform investments to goals.
- Drive **execution excellence** — processes, priorities, predictable delivery as the org scales.
- Track emerging tech (**Web3, cloud, AI**) → actionable technical strategy.
- Build, mentor, retain **engineering leaders and ICs**; raise the technical bar; coaching.
- Own **hiring strategy & workforce planning**; recruit top talent, develop future leaders.
- Shape **engineering culture** — standards, mentorship, continuous-learning programs.

**What they want:**
- BS CS/Eng or equivalent experience.
- **10+ yrs software engineering, deep distributed-systems expertise.**
- **5+ yrs engineering leadership incl. managing managers**, leading orgs for critical production infra at a
  **high-growth startup**.
- Hands-on large-scale distributed systems + cloud infra.
- **Java, Kubernetes, AWS, multi-region, multi-cloud** strongly preferred.
- Judgment balancing speed/quality/long-term health; strong written+verbal comms; engage the **open-source
  community**; ownership mindset; comfortable with ambiguity; ship pragmatically.
- Passion for platforms that enable others; curiosity about crypto, AI, complex systems.
- **Nice to have:** developer platforms / API products; crypto/blockchain/adjacent infra; founder/startup leadership.
