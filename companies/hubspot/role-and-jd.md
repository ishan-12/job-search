## The Role (from the JD)

Lead HubSpot's Infrastructure Engineering org — shared platforms, services, and tooling: cloud infrastructure,
developer platforms, data infrastructure, reliability engineering, observability. Define how infrastructure
evolves in an AI-first company.

**Key responsibilities:** long-term infra vision/strategy; lead & develop a high-performing org with succession
planning; evolve cloud infra, PaaS, and data infrastructure; **lead the AI-first transformation** (where AI
changes how software is built and operated); global infra expansion with reliability & security; partner with
Product/Eng/Security/executives; champion **SRE** & operational excellence; foster technical excellence; build
relationships with **hyperscale cloud providers**.

**Required:** executive leadership of large multi-layer orgs; building Infra/Platform/Cloud-Platform orgs in
modern SaaS; **globally distributed systems at internet scale (millions of users)**; cloud-native + distributed
systems; technical strategy → business outcomes; developing engineering leaders; executive communication;
**passion for leveraging AI to transform engineering**.
**Nice-to-have:** modern databases / large-scale data platforms; internal developer platforms; strategic
partnerships with AWS/GCP/Azure; cloud cost optimization at enterprise scale; **community engagement (writing/
speaking/open-source)**.
