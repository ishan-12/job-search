## Full JD (captured from posting)

**Team & mission:** Figma's AI Tools team builds AI-powered workflows, platforms, and tooling that
make every engineer at the company more productive. The team owns a three-layer platform stack —
**sandbox runtime, cloud agents, and workflow orchestration** — serving hundreds of engineers org-wide.

### Responsibilities
- Lead and expand an engineering team building Figma's AI developer workflows and cloud agent platform.
- Own technical strategy and roadmap across sandbox runtime, cloud agent reliability, workflow orchestration, and agentic workflows.
- Hire and scale the team; establish culture and operational processes.
- Drive platform reliability, observability, and scalability as adoption grows.
- Partner cross-functionally to identify high-leverage AI-assisted workflow opportunities.
- Evaluate emerging AI tools, models, and agent frameworks for **build-vs-buy** decisions.
- Create **evaluation frameworks measuring quality, cost, and impact of AI-generated code and agentic workflows**.
- Coach engineers through career development and technical leadership.

### Required
- 3+ years managing infrastructure, platform, or developer-experience engineering teams, with scaling experience.
- Staff-level-or-above technical background; deep knowledge of distributed systems and production infrastructure.
- Fluency with AI/ML systems, LLM workflows, or agent architectures **as a builder**.
- Experience hiring and growing teams in high-growth environments.
- Ability to set technical direction and drive cross-functional alignment.

### Preferred
- Building AI developer tooling or agent platforms.
- Familiarity with **Cursor, Claude Code, or MCP integrations**.
- Workflow orchestration platforms (Temporal, n8n, Tines).
- Developer-experience platform background (CI/CD, build tools, monorepo environments).
- AI evaluation and quality-framework experience.
