## Risks / concerns / open questions

1. **The posting itself may not be genuine.** See the ⚠️ section in
   `role-and-jd.md` — job-ID format, vague location, and an unusually high comp
   band all point to an aggregator rewrite or a fabrication. **Verify on
   medtronic.com/careers before submitting anything.**
2. **Scope ambiguity.** How many engineers? Any budget ownership? Is this a
   platform-owning role or a delivery-management role inside central IT?
3. **Internal enterprise IT vs product platform.** "Supports enterprise
   technology initiatives" reads as corporate IT. Ishan's recent work is
   customer-facing product platform. Confirm which one this is — it changes
   whether the role is a step forward or sideways.
4. **Validated-system burden.** If this role owns validated (GxP / 21 CFR Part 11)
   infrastructure, there is a real learning curve. Ask directly.
5. **Comp reality.** If the $280–345K band is aggregator fiction, the real band
   may be materially lower. Establish this early rather than late.
6. **Pace.** Big-medtech release cadence vs. startup velocity is a genuine
   culture question, not a throwaway one.
